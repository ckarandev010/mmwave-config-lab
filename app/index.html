Positions in metres: x sideways, y straight out from the radar, z height above the floor. Heading 0° walks straight at the radar. Detection, noise and tracking are a simplified model meant to show trends, not to predict exact numbers.

Open source. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Texas Instruments. Argument rules come from the mmWave SDK 3.6 and Radar Toolbox sources; physics uses the standard FMCW formulas. A config that passes here can still fail on hardware, so test on a bench before deploying.
